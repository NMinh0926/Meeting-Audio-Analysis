# Emotion per turn and per speaker: text model, voice model, combination (2026-10-01)

Why: the user wants each speaker labelled like "Người A – Nam – Vui vẻ". The earlier per-turn sentiment
(`lxyuan/distilbert-base-multilingual-cased-sentiments-student`, positive / negative / neutral mapped to
happy / angry / neutral) had never been measured and labelled many turns "angry".

App emotions (`app/services/emotion_labels.py`): neutral, happy, sad, angry, surprised, fearful.

## 1. Text models on UIT-VSMEC
`python -m scripts.bench_emotion_text MODEL…`: the 693-sentence test split of UIT-VSMEC
(`tridm/UIT-VSMEC`, Vietnamese Facebook comments, 7 labels; Disgust folded into angry, Other into neutral).
Reference: neutral 129, happy 193, sad 116, angry 172, surprised 37, fearful 46. CPU.

| Model | Licence | Accuracy | Macro-F1 | Predicted (neu/hap/sad/ang/sur/fea) | ms / sentence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lxyuan multilingual sentiment (before) | Apache-2.0 | 33.2 % | 16.1 % | 28/197/0/**468**/0/0 | 20 |
| visolex/emotion-sphobert | Apache-2.0 | 24.8 % | 6.6 % | 0/0/0/693/0/0 | 31 |
| visolex/bartpho-emotion | Apache-2.0 | 62.2 % | 59.2 % | 144/187/120/182/17/43 | 122 |
| **HalogenFlo/phobert-vsmec-emotion-recognition** | MIT | **68.3 %** | **68.4 %** | 139/197/107/157/34/59 | 32 |

- The earlier model calls 468 of 693 sentences angry, as seen in the meetings.
- `emotion-sphobert`'s weights do not load into the standard RoBERTa class (every layer reported
  missing), so it predicts at random; excluded. `ThuanPhong/phobertbase-finetuned-vsmec` has no licence;
  not tested.
- HalogenFlo's own card reports 61.5 % accuracy on 7 labels; 68.3 % here is on 6 (disgust + anger merged).
  Its training split is not documented, so test-set overlap cannot be ruled out.
- Used sentence by sentence (VSMEC is single sentences), averaged over a turn by sentence duration.

## 2. Voice model: emotion2vec+ large
`emotion2vec/emotion2vec_plus_large` via `funasr` (FunASR model licence: free use, keep source and model
name). 9 labels, multilingual training; no Vietnamese emotional speech with a usable licence was found to
score it (the one public set, `slexisvn/emotion-tts-vietnamese-sample`, uses Google TTS voice names, so
it was not used). Memory grows with the square of the input length: a 4-minute turn needed > 3 GB and
failed, so turns are cut into pieces of at most 10 s.

**False emotions on calm read speech** — `python -m scripts.bench_emotion_neutral`: the 120 FLEURS
clips (read Wikipedia sentences; neutral is the expected answer), voice weight 0.6:

| Side | Voice "disgusted" mapped to | Neutral | False emotion | happy | sad | angry | surprised | fearful |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| voice | angry | 97/120 | 19.2 % | 4 | 7 | 11 | 1 | 0 |
| text | – | 76/120 | 36.7 % | 22 | 5 | 11 | 1 | 5 |
| combined | angry | 100/120 | 16.7 % | 5 | 2 | 11 | 1 | 1 |
| voice | **neutral** | 109/120 | 9.2 % | 4 | 6 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| **combined** | **neutral** | **110/120** | **8.3 %** | 5 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

All 11 false "angry" from the voice came from its "disgusted" label, so voice disgust now counts as
neutral (text disgust still counts as anger).

## 3. Combination
`combined = 0.6 × voice + 0.4 × text` over the six emotions. The voice carries the tone; the text model is
often very confident on plain statements (e.g. "Năm.", "Hạt phô tông thậm chí còn nhỏ hơn…" → angry
0.94–0.97). On 51 turns of the test meetings, voice and text agreed on 22; the weights are a judgement,
**not tuned on labelled data** (none exists for Vietnamese speech). Speaker emotion = the emotion that
fills most of the speaker's talk time.

After `scripts.recompute_emotions` on the stored meetings: FLEURS test meetings — every speaker neutral;
Life Abroad podcasts — guests happy (ep4: 67 % and 96 % of talk time; ep8: 76 %), hosts mostly neutral;
3–5 angry turns out of 81–138 per episode, all one-word turns (none left after 3b).

## 3b. Turns too short to hear (fix after user report)
The user reported "Năm." (0.28 s, ep4 5 min at 2:55) labelled angry. Turns under EMOTION_MIN_SECONDS (0.5 s)
get no voice piece, so the text model decided alone. Over the 294 stored turns:

| | Short turns (< 0.5 s) | neutral | happy | angry | surprised |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Text alone (before) | 15 | 6 | 2 | **6** ("Cái", "Đối", "cho", "Năm.") | 1 |
| **Silent voice side counts as neutral** | 15 | **15** | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Only those 9 turns changed (`scripts.recompute_emotions`); with the voice weight at 0.6 a single word can
no longer outvote it. A voice model *failure* still falls back to text alone. FLEURS clips (5–20 s) are
unaffected. All 294 turns now: neutral 177, happy 113, sad 4.

## Decision
Text: HalogenFlo PhoBERT (VSMEC) per sentence on CPU. Voice: emotion2vec+ large on the GPU in ≤ 10 s
pieces, "disgusted" as neutral. Combined 0.6 / 0.4. Next step if accuracy matters: label ~50 real meeting
turns and tune the weights against them.
