# Whisper decoding options: missed sentences vs hallucinations (2026-10-01)

Why: after switching to `large-v3-turbo` + VAD, the male sentence at 21.8–30.2 s of
`fleurs_meeting_2_1nam_3nu.mp3` was missing from the transcript in every run. With VAD off it came back,
but Whisper added a YouTube-style outro that nobody said ("Hãy subscribe cho kênh …").

## Setup
- `large-v3-turbo`, `int8_float16`, `word_timestamps=True`, RTX 3050 Ti, worker stopped.
- 10 synthetic meetings of 4 held-out FLEURS voices (2 M / 2 F), gaps of 0.2 s and 0.8 s alternating.
- Missed clip: transcript words cover < 30 % of a clip. Hallucinated segment: < 30 % of it inside any clip.

## Results

| Decoding | Missed clips | Hallucinated segments | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| a) VAD default (was in use) | 1/40 | 1 | "Hãy subscribe cho kênh lalaschool …" |
| b) no VAD | 0/40 | 1 | "Cảm ơn các bạn đã theo dõi." |
| c) no VAD + `hallucination_silence_threshold=2` | 0/40 | 1 | "Hẹn gặp lại các bạn trong những video tiếp theo." |
| d) VAD, min silence 500 ms, pad 200 ms | 0/40 | 0 | |
| e) VAD + `condition_on_previous_text=False` | 1/40 | 0 | |
| **f) no VAD + threshold 2 s + `condition_on_previous_text=False`** | **0/40** | **0** | |

Confirmation of (d) and (f):

| | (d) | (f) |
|---|---|---|
| User test meetings (3 × 4 voices), missed | 1/12 (meeting 2 again) | **0/12** |
| Podcast excerpt ending mid-speech | no hallucination | no hallucination |
| FLEURS 120-clip WER | 5.8 % | 5.9 % |

## Decision
(f): no VAD filter, `hallucination_silence_threshold = WHISPER_HALLUCINATION_SILENCE_SECONDS` (2 s),
`condition_on_previous_text=False`. Losing a real sentence is worse than a stray outro, and (f) avoids both
on these sets. Side effect seen on the podcast: final punctuation is sometimes missing.
